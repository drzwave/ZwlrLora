7 Aug 2026 Mt Agamenticus Maine

The goal was to find a distant spot that had a direct LoS view of the top of the mountain which is easily identifable by the large cell tower.
But we could not find a place that wasn't surrounded with trees that seemed to block the signal enough that it didn't make connection.

ZWLR is a little suspect as the antenna has loosened up from all the carting around while stuck on the end of a long pole.

HaLow performed the best in this location making connection even over the top of the hill.
The static point is not at the very top of the hill but in the corner of the parking lot near the cell tower.
The first distant location is the parking lot at the base of the mountain which does not have a clear LoS to the top but the parking lot is open. Halow was barely able to make connection here.
The second distant location was Algonquin Dr to the east since the static location is on the east side of the mountain. Algonquin Dr is on a small ridge and is visible from the summit but it is surrounded with dense tall pin trees.
The third location is along Chases pond which is also visible from the summit to the south east. But the section of the pond near a road does not have LoS to the summit.

Using google earth it looks like Old Mountain road might have a view of the summit.
In the distance is Zoes Way which is 4 miles away.
If I end up doing this again I'll have to try this out.
