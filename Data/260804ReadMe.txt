2026-08-04 Data capture in Rochester NH
This location has the controllers next to the Rochester Fairgrounds which provides an open field in one direction and a dense suburban neighborhood on the other side.
Weather: Clear skies, temperatures in the 80s in the morning then low 90s by the afternoon.
The units mounted on the car roof got very warm which may have impacted overall RF range as on previous runs in the morning the range was further.

All 3 protocols were tested simultaneously on an eBike in Rochester NH.

The challenge has been HaLow tends to drop and often doesn't reconnect.
Thus, the data is somewhat limited.
In the afternoon I had chatgpt improve the gpsServer.py script to make it more robust and recover from many Exceptions but something still drops the connection.

LoRa and ZWLR both suffered from interference from HaLow or possibly the RPi.

LoRa/ZWLR were captured on a 2nd pass with HaLow (and RPi) powered off.
The 2nd pass capture was a somewhat shorter path mostly along the outer edge of range.
