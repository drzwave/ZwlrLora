After several attempts to get a complete data capture and determining the general range of all three protocols,
the final data capture was performed in the evening when the car was in the shadow of nearby trees and buildings.
Temperature was 89F with clear skies.
HaLow specifically kept failing somewhere along the capture. The final data was one of the best captures.

The range is pretty good considering the size of many brick buildings surrounding the parking lot.
The fixed location is next to the police department which has a large antenna on the roof.
There is lots of noise in the 880MHz band from cellular LTE traffic.

Z-Wave JS reports a high noise floor in the City of -78dBm.
Most places have a noise floor of the limit of the chip at around -108.

There is LTE cell phone traffic in the 880MHz band which can be seen in the spectrum analyzer screenshot.
